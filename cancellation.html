Cancellation of Paid Services

Users can cancel their subscription or paid services with Lala Stories at any time. The cancellation will be effective at the end of the current billing cycle.

Users will continue to have access to all paid features until the end of the current billing period, even after cancellation is submitted.

Lala Stories does not provide pro-rated refunds for unused portions of the subscription or service.

To cancel, manage your subscription through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android) — the same platform where your subscription was originally purchased.

Refunds

Refunds are typically processed in cases of accidental overcharges, billing errors, or any other issues arising from Lala Stories' end.

Refunds are subject to review and approval by Lala Stories and are issued at their discretion.

In-app purchases, if applicable, are typically subject to the respective app store's refund policies (Apple App Store / Google Play Store).

Refunds for content purchases (e.g., virtual gifts, premium content) are generally not provided unless there is a technical issue or a mistake on Lala Stories' part.

Subscription Renewals

Subscriptions are renewed automatically unless canceled by the user before the renewal date.

Refunds are not typically provided for subscription renewals if the user fails to cancel before the renewal date.

Users should manage their subscriptions through the app store or platform where the subscription was originally purchased (Apple App Store or Google Play Store).

We recommend turning on renewal reminders in your device settings or app store account to avoid unexpected charges.
